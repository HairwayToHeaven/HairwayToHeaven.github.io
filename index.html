Meet your hair artist

Hi, I'm Andrea. I can't wait to meet you.

I've always believed hair is about so much more than hair. It's how you meet the mirror each morning, how you walk into a room, how you feel about the person looking back at you. When your hair feels right, everything feels a little more possible.

Hairway to Heaven is my golden little sanctuary: a calm, joyful space where you can slow down, breathe and be properly looked after. I take my time, I use gentle, planet-friendly products, and I pour my whole heart into every cut and color, because you deserve to feel wonderful. And when a big day comes along, I'll bring all that love straight to you.

With love, Andrea

Our eco promise

Beautiful hair, gentle footprint.

Like the pines and clear blue water around her, Andrea believes beauty shines brightest when it's natural and well cared for.

Looking after you and looking after our planet go hand in hand. Here's how Hairway to Heaven treads lightly.

Plant-powered products

Vegan, cruelty-free care and color, chosen to be kind to your hair, your scalp and the earth.

Recycle & refill

Foils, color tubes and packaging recycled, with refillable bottles wherever we can.

Water-wise washing

Mindful rinsing that saves water without skimping on that blissful, bubbly wash.

Mindful every day

Small, loving choices, from laundered towels to low-energy tools, that add up to a gentler salon.
